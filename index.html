How scoring works
  • Hit every dot. Each dot is an equal share of the 1000 points, so one more dot is worth more than a slightly shorter line.
  • Short lines score best. Up to par the length is free. After that points drop slowly (×0.97 at 125% of par, ×0.92 at 150%, ×0.76 at 200%), and lines that wiggle all over the screen score nothing.
  • Stars are for the line. They need every dot and no red wall crossed: ★★★ within 5% of par, ★★ within 25%, ★ within 41%.
  • Par is the length of our reference answer. Parts of the graph that leave the screen count their width, and jumps count their height.
How scoring works
  • Every dot counts. Score = 1000 × dots hit ÷ dots; you don't need them all.
  • Short formula. Each number, letter (x, a–d, π, e), operator and function counts 1 (brackets are free). The first 15 symbols are free; a few more cost a little, many more cost a lot.
  • Short line. Up to the dot-to-dot length it’s free. After that points drop slowly (×0.92 at 1.5×, ×0.76 at 2×, ×0.37 at 3×). Crossing a red wall in view halves the score. ★ 60% of the bot’s score, ★★ 85%, ★★★ match or beat it.
